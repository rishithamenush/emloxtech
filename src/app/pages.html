EmloX Tech · Company privacy

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how EmloX Tech handles personal information in business communications, project enquiries, client engagements, and use of our website. For privacy questions, contact info@emloxtech.com.

Information you provide

When you contact or work with EmloX Tech, you may provide your name, business contact details, company information, project requirements, and correspondence. Our website form also allows you to share an optional budget. We use this information to respond to your questions, assess requirements, prepare proposals, and communicate about agreed work. Please avoid including passwords, payment details, or confidential client data in your first message.

How enquiries are handled

When you use direct submission, your details pass through our hosting provider, Vercel, and our email delivery provider, Resend, to our business inbox. If delivery is unavailable, you can send the brief through your own email service or download a copy. Preparing or downloading a brief alone does not send an enquiry.

Information in client projects

A project may involve information supplied by a client about its users or business. The relevant project agreement and any data-processing terms define the scope, permitted use, access, and handling of that information. This general policy does not replace those project-specific terms.

Website operation

Vercel serves this website and may process technical request information such as IP addresses and browser details for delivery, reliability, and security. The website does not add advertising trackers or marketing cookies. Form drafts are held in the current page and are not saved to a website account or database.

Access and retention

Business contact details and project correspondence are used to manage enquiries and client relationships. Email and hosting providers process information as part of delivering those services and may process it in other countries. We retain information as needed for the enquiry, the agreed engagement, and applicable record-keeping requirements.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information by emailing info@emloxtech.com. We will consider requests under applicable data-protection requirements, including any records we need to retain.

External links

External websites and services have their own privacy notices. This policy describes EmloX Tech’s handling of personal information; it does not govern independent third parties.

Changes

We update this notice when our company’s data-handling practices change.
