WEBSITE PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

This notice explains how EmloX Tech handles information through this website and project enquiries. For questions, contact info@emloxtech.com.

Information you provide

The enquiry form asks for your name, email address, selected service, and project description. Company and budget are optional. We use this information to respond, discuss your requirements, and prepare a proposal. Please avoid including passwords, payment details, or confidential client data in your first message.

How enquiries are handled

When you use direct submission, your details pass through our hosting provider, Vercel, and our email delivery provider, Resend, to our business inbox. If delivery is unavailable, you can send the brief through your own email service or download a copy. Preparing or downloading a brief alone does not send an enquiry.

Website operation

Vercel serves this website and may process technical request information such as IP addresses and browser details for delivery, reliability, and security. The website does not add advertising trackers or marketing cookies. Form drafts are held in the current page and are not saved to a website account or database.

Access and retention

Enquiry information is used for business correspondence and project planning. Email and hosting providers process information as part of delivering those services and may process it in other countries. We retain correspondence as needed for the enquiry, any resulting engagement, and applicable record-keeping requirements.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your enquiry information by emailing info@emloxtech.com. We will consider requests under applicable data-protection requirements, including any records we need to retain.

External links

External websites have their own privacy notices. This policy covers the EmloX Tech website.

Changes

We update this notice when the website’s data-handling practices change.
